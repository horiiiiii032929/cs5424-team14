\needspace{4\baselineskip}\section{Task 1: Baseline design for workload A}
\needspace{4\baselineskip}\subsection*{\texttt{expt\_\allowbreak{}a0}: baseline protocol and results}
expt\_a0 ran as Slurm job 918524 on five xcnf nodes, with xcnf8 as coordinator and four workers. It used PostgreSQL 18.6/Citus 14.1.0, scale 1, SERIALIZABLE isolation, 60-second warmup, 600-second measurements and full log sampling. The A transaction weights T1-T9 were 36/20/10/10/10/5/5/2/2 percent. Baseline work\_mem was 32MB, shared\_buffers 1GB and executor pool limit 4.\par
At 1/4/8/16/32/64 clients, baseline TPS was 3.220/2.283/2.285/2.368/2.405/2.283 respectively. p95 latency rose from 2654.656 ms at one client to 180370.276 ms at 64; p99 rose from 2698.524 to 187011.576 ms. pgbench exited zero in all six cases, but the last three had 25/51/50 serialization failures. Those failures must remain visible in comparisons.\par
At one client, T3 averaged 2678.797 ms, T4 458.862 ms and T7 51.114 ms. Weighting each transaction's observed mean latency by its completed count, T3/T4 accounted for about 93.6\% of recorded time at one client and 97.2\% at 64 clients. These are time shares, not workload weights. Throughput did not scale with more clients, so placement and the dominant reads take priority over short update transactions.\par
The baseline distributes orders and order\_items by different generated IDs, and inventory by inventory\_id. The relevant joins do not consistently match those keys. Fresh a0 control 977740 on xcnf0-4 completed 3.225/3.565/3.568/3.282/3.613/3.222 TPS at 1/4/8/16/32/64 clients, with 0/0/2/20/64/59 serialization failures. This is 41-56\% above preserved baseline 918524 at 4+ clients; the cause is not established. Optimized a112 is compared against this fresh control on the same nodes.\par
