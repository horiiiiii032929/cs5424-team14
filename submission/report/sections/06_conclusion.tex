\needspace{4\baselineskip}\section{Conclusion}
The combined A design a112 uses lookup indexes, join-key colocation and READ ONLY declarations; matched runs delivered 16.8-28.2\ensuremath{\times} fresh baseline throughput. a115's complete audit verifies faster eligible observations, seven phase-complete cases (six with zero failures), all 12 passing validations, and repeated measurement/warmup aborts. For Workload B, the matched experiment showed that b6 achieved 20.86 times the baseline TPS at 1 client. However, aborted runs and serialization failures still limit comparisons at higher client counts. The A pair selected a112; a115 is follow-up work and a10 remains separate. Final B selection remains with the B pair. All abort/retry and correctness limitations are retained.\par
Once joins improved, T6 became the main cost. Pre-aggregation is not automatically faster: a114's maintained summaries slowed T6 in the A diagnostic, while a115's timestamp index improved it. Isolated controls, repeated matched runs and explicit abort/retry reporting are essential; passing integrity checks do not make an aborted benchmark clean.\par
